Our Story

From a Small Repair Shop to a Premium Bicycle Destination

In 2010, two passionate cyclists — Rajesh Kumar and his childhood friend — opened a modest bicycle repair shop in the heart of the city. What started as a humble garage workspace with basic tools and an unwavering love for two-wheelers has since transformed into one of India's most respected bicycle retail destinations.

The early days were defined by long hours, greasy hands, and a deep commitment to getting every repair right. Customers didn't just come for fixes — they came for honest advice, genuine parts, and the kind of care you'd only find from someone who truly understands bicycles. Word spread quickly, and the shop became a gathering point for local cycling enthusiasts.

By 2013, the demand for quality bicycles — not just repairs — was undeniable. We expanded into retail, partnering with five leading brands and curating a selection of road bikes, mountain bikes, hybrid commuters, and kids' cycles. The response was overwhelming, and we realized that cyclists across the country deserved better access to premium products and expert guidance.

Our Mission & Values

The principles that guide everything we do at CycleHub

Our Mission

"To make cycling accessible, enjoyable, and sustainable for everyone."

We believe cycling is more than transportation — it's a lifestyle that promotes health, happiness, and environmental responsibility. Our mission is to remove every barrier between a person and the open road, from stocking bicycles at every price point to offering free fit sessions and community group rides.

Our Vision

"To become India's most trusted bicycle destination."

We envision a future where choosing a bicycle is as exciting and informed as choosing a car. A future where every cyclist — regardless of experience or budget — has access to the best products, the best advice, and the best service in the country. We're building India's most comprehensive cycling ecosystem.

Our Values

"Integrity, passion, and community in everything we do."

From stocking bicycles at every price point to offering free fit sessions and community group rides, we actively work to make cycling approachable for beginners and rewarding for veterans. Every product we sell and every interaction we have is guided by these core values.
